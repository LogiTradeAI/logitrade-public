# LogiTrade Brand Guidelines

## Introduction

The LogiTrade brand represents intelligent automation, artificial intelligence, and responsible trading technology.

The visual identity is designed to communicate:

* Intelligence.
* Reliability.
* Innovation.
* Precision.
* Trust.

These guidelines help maintain a consistent brand experience across all platforms.

---

# Brand Identity

## Brand Name

Official name:

**LogiTrade**

The name represents:

* Logic-driven decision making.
* Intelligent trading systems.
* Technology-assisted market analysis.

---

# Brand Personality

LogiTrade communicates with a style that is:

## Intelligent

Focused on advanced technology and AI-driven solutions.

## Professional

Designed for serious users who value structured decision-making.

## Transparent

Clear about capabilities, limitations, and risks.

## Innovative

Focused on improving how users interact with financial technology.

---

# Visual Direction

The LogiTrade visual style should communicate:

* Advanced AI technology.
* Modern financial infrastructure.
* Premium SaaS experience.
* Data-driven intelligence.

The design language should balance:

* Technology.
* Simplicity.
* Trust.
* Performance.

# Typography

Typography should communicate:

* Clarity.
* Professionalism.
* Modern technology.

Recommended characteristics:

* Clean sans-serif fonts.
* Strong readability.
* Clear hierarchy.

Avoid:

* Decorative fonts.
* Difficult-to-read styles.
* Excessive visual effects.

---

# Logo Guidelines

The LogiTrade logo should:

* Maintain original proportions.
* Preserve readability.
* Have sufficient surrounding space.
* Avoid unauthorized modifications.

Do not:

* Stretch the logo.
* Change official colors.
* Add unapproved effects.
* Rotate the logo.
* Place it on unsuitable backgrounds.

---

# Logo Variations

Approved logo variations may include:

* Primary logo.
* Dark background version.
* Light background version.
* Icon-only version.

Official files should be stored in:

```text
branding/logos/
```

---

# Product Screenshots

Screenshots should represent:

* Accurate product functionality.
* Current interface design.
* Professional presentation.

Screenshots should not display:

* Real user information.
* Private account data.
* Sensitive trading information.

Stored in:

```text
branding/screenshots/
```

---

# Marketing Language Guidelines

LogiTrade communication should focus on:

* AI-assisted trading technology.
* Automation.
* Risk awareness.
* Intelligent analysis.

Avoid statements suggesting:

* Guaranteed profits.
* Risk-free trading.
* Certain investment outcomes.

---

# Brand Positioning

LogiTrade is positioned as:

"An AI-powered trading technology platform designed to help users analyze markets, automate workflows, and make structured trading decisions."

---

# Future Brand Expansion

As LogiTrade develops, branding may expand into:

* Mobile applications.
* Partner materials.
* Developer resources.
* Educational content.
* Community assets.

All expansions should maintain the core identity principles.
