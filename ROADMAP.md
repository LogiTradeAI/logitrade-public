# LogiTrade Roadmap

## Overview

The LogiTrade roadmap outlines the development journey toward building an AI-powered cryptocurrency trading platform designed to provide intelligent market analysis, automated decision-making, and advanced risk management.

The roadmap is focused on building a reliable foundation through research, testing, security improvements, and continuous development before expanding access to a wider audience.

---

# Phase 1 — Research & Foundation

**Status: Completed / Ongoing**

### Objectives

Build the foundation required for an AI-driven trading ecosystem.

### Development Areas

* Research cryptocurrency market behavior.
* Analyze historical market data.
* Study different trading approaches and portfolio structures.
* Test various signals and market indicators.
* Explore AI-assisted trading methodologies.
* Establish initial system architecture.

### Goals

Create the knowledge foundation required for intelligent automated trading.

# Development Philosophy

LogiTrade prioritizes:

* Data-driven improvement.
* Responsible automation.
* Continuous testing.
* Security-first development.
* Long-term user value.

The goal is not to create a system that predicts every market movement, but to build an intelligent assistant that helps users make more informed and disciplined trading decisions.
