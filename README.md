# LogiTrade

> AI-Powered Cryptocurrency Trading Platform

LogiTrade is an AI-powered Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed to simplify and automate cryptocurrency trading through a coordinated network of specialized AI models.

Built on years of market research, portfolio analysis, signal testing, and historical data, LogiTrade helps users participate in the cryptocurrency market without requiring extensive trading experience. The platform continuously analyzes opportunities across multiple assets and manages trades from entry to take profit with risk management at its core.

## Vision

To make intelligent, accessible, and responsible trading available to everyone through artificial intelligence.

## What LogiTrade Does

* Monitors multiple cryptocurrency markets in real time.
* Evaluates potential trading opportunities.
* Performs AI-assisted risk analysis before execution.
* Automates trade management from entry to exit.
* Minimizes unnecessary losses through disciplined risk controls.
* Provides a user-friendly experience for both new and experienced traders.

## AI Ecosystem

LogiTrade coordinates multiple specialized AI systems:

* **GPT** — Strategy generation and optimization
* **Claude** — Auditing and validation
* **Gemini** — Visual chart analysis
* **DeepSeek** — Logical reasoning
* **Grok** — Real-time market sentiment
* **LogiTrade Core** — Trade orchestration and execution
