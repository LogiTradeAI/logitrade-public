# LogiTrade Company Information

## Introduction

LogiTrade is an artificial intelligence-powered cryptocurrency trading technology company focused on building intelligent systems that help users navigate complex digital asset markets.

The company develops AI-driven solutions designed to analyze market information, evaluate trading opportunities, manage risk, and simplify the trading experience through automation.

LogiTrade was created with the belief that advanced technology can help reduce the complexity of cryptocurrency trading by providing users with intelligent tools, structured analysis, and automated assistance.
