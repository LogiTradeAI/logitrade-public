# LogiTrade Target Users

## Overview

LogiTrade is designed for individuals who want to participate in cryptocurrency markets with the assistance of artificial intelligence, automated analysis, and structured risk management.

The platform is built for users who want to reduce the complexity of trading, save time on market analysis, and make more informed decisions through AI-powered trading intelligence.

LogiTrade is not designed to replace user responsibility. Instead, it provides technology that assists users in navigating complex market conditions.

# Primary Users

## 1. New Cryptocurrency Traders

### Profile

Individuals who are interested in cryptocurrency trading but lack the experience, knowledge, or time required to analyze markets manually.

### Challenges

New traders often struggle with:

* Understanding technical analysis.
* Identifying quality trading opportunities.
* Managing emotions during trades.
* Learning effective risk management.
* Spending significant time studying charts.

### How LogiTrade Helps

LogiTrade provides:

* AI-assisted market analysis.
* Automated trade evaluation.
* Simplified trading workflows.
* Risk-focused decision support.

The platform helps reduce the complexity of entering cryptocurrency markets.
