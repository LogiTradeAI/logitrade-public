# LogiTrade Subscription Model

## Introduction

LogiTrade operates as a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform that provides users access to AI-powered cryptocurrency trading intelligence, automated analysis tools, and platform capabilities.

The subscription model is designed to provide users with access to continuously improving technology, AI systems, infrastructure, and trading assistance features.

Users subscribe to access the LogiTrade platform and its services. A subscription does not represent payment for guaranteed returns, guaranteed profits, or financial outcomes.

---

# Subscription Philosophy

LogiTrade is built around the principle that advanced trading technology should be accessible through a transparent and structured service model.

The subscription provides access to:

* AI-powered market analysis.
* Automated trading capabilities.
* Platform features.
* Risk management tools.
* Continuous system improvements.
* Future platform enhancements.

The purpose of the subscription is to support the development, operation, and improvement of the LogiTrade ecosystem.
