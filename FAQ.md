# LogiTrade FAQ

Frequently asked questions about LogiTrade, its AI-powered trading service, and how the platform works.

---

# What is LogiTrade?

LogiTrade is an AI-powered cryptocurrency trading SaaS platform designed to automate market analysis, trade evaluation, and decision-making through a network of specialized AI models.

The platform analyzes market conditions, evaluates opportunities, manages risk, and assists with automated trading processes.

---

# How does LogiTrade work?

LogiTrade uses multiple AI systems, each assigned a specific role in the trading process.

The system analyzes market data, evaluates potential opportunities, performs risk assessment, and determines whether a trade meets predefined criteria before execution.

The AI models work together under the LogiTrade decision engine to create a structured trading workflow.

---

# Does LogiTrade guarantee profits?

No.

No trading system can guarantee profits. Cryptocurrency markets are highly dynamic and unpredictable.

LogiTrade is designed to improve decision-making, manage risk, and reduce unnecessary losses through AI-assisted analysis and disciplined trading processes.

---

# Is cryptocurrency trading risk-free with LogiTrade?

No.

Trading always involves risk, including the possibility of losing capital.

LogiTrade focuses on risk management, position evaluation, and adaptive decision-making to help manage exposure, but losses remain possible.

# Who is LogiTrade designed for?

LogiTrade is designed for users who want to participate in cryptocurrency markets without spending years learning complex trading analysis.

It may be useful for:

* New traders learning about automated trading.
* Users who do not have time to monitor markets continuously.
* Experienced traders looking for AI-assisted decision support.
