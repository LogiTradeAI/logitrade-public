# Cryptocurrency Market Risk

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile and can experience significant price movements within short periods.

Market prices may change rapidly due to:

* Economic events.
* Regulatory announcements.
* Industry developments.
* Investor behavior.
* Global financial conditions.
* Unexpected events.
