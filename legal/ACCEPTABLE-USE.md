# LogiTrade Acceptable Use Policy

## Effective Date

2026

---

# Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") defines the permitted and prohibited uses of LogiTrade services.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that LogiTrade remains a secure, reliable, and responsible AI-powered trading technology platform for all users.

By accessing or using LogiTrade, users agree to follow this Acceptable Use Policy.

This policy applies to all users, accounts, integrations, and interactions with LogiTrade services.

---

# Purpose of Acceptable Use

LogiTrade is designed to provide:

* AI-powered market analysis.
* Automated trading technology.
* Trading workflow assistance.
* Risk management tools.
* Software-based financial technology services.

Users are expected to use the platform responsibly and only for legitimate purposes.

---

# General User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

* Maintaining account security.
* Using the platform lawfully.
* Understanding platform limitations.
* Following applicable regulations.
* Protecting access credentials.
* Using services according to intended functionality.

Users should not use LogiTrade in ways that may harm the platform, other users, or third parties.

# Prohibited Uses

Users must not use LogiTrade for activities that violate laws, regulations, or platform security.

---

# Unauthorized Access

Users must not attempt to:

* Access another user's account.
* Bypass authentication systems.
* Obtain unauthorized access to platform systems.
* Circumvent security controls.

---

# Reverse Engineering and System Analysis

Users must not:

* Reverse engineer LogiTrade software.
* Attempt to extract proprietary algorithms.
* Decompile platform components.
* Discover confidential system architecture.
* Recreate internal AI workflows.

LogiTrade contains proprietary technology that is protected by intellectual property rights.

---

# Abuse of AI Systems

Users must not intentionally misuse LogiTrade AI systems.

Prohibited activities include:

* Attempting to manipulate AI outputs.
* Testing systems through unauthorized abuse.
* Flooding AI services with excessive requests.
* Attempting to extract confidential AI behavior.
* Using automated methods to exploit platform responses.

---

# Excessive Resource Usage

Users must not:

* Overload platform infrastructure.
* Create excessive automated requests.
* Circumvent usage limits.
* Consume resources beyond intended usage.

LogiTrade may restrict access to protect platform stability.

---

# Data Scraping and Collection

Users must not:

* Scrape platform data without permission.
* Collect proprietary information automatically.
* Extract system outputs for unauthorized purposes.
* Build competing services using LogiTrade data.

---

# Account Sharing

Users should not share accounts unless explicitly permitted.

Account sharing may create:

* Security risks.
* Unauthorized access concerns.
* Privacy issues.
* Subscription misuse.

Users are responsible for all activity performed through their accounts.

Users must not use LogiTrade for:

* Fraud.
* Market manipulation.
* Misleading activities.
* Illegal financial activities.
* Unauthorized transactions.

Users are responsible for ensuring their activities comply with applicable laws.

Users must not attempt to compromise LogiTrade security.
