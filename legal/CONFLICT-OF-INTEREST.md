# LogiTrade Conflict of Interest Policy

## Effective Date

2026

---

# Introduction

This Conflict of Interest Policy ("Policy") describes how LogiTrade identifies, manages, and reduces potential conflicts of interest that may arise from operating an artificial intelligence-powered cryptocurrency trading technology platform.

LogiTrade is committed to maintaining transparency, fairness, and responsible technology development.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that platform decisions are designed around user trust, system integrity, and responsible operation.

---

# Purpose of This Policy

As a technology company operating within the cryptocurrency industry, LogiTrade recognizes that potential conflicts of interest may exist.

These may involve:

* Business relationships.
* Technology providers.
* Trading environments.
* Partnerships.
* Revenue incentives.
* Internal decision-making.

LogiTrade aims to identify and manage these situations appropriately.

---

# Company Role

LogiTrade provides software technology, artificial intelligence systems, and trading automation tools.

LogiTrade does not represent itself as a guaranteed source of financial success.

The platform is designed to:

* Provide AI-assisted analysis.
* Automate defined trading workflows.
* Assist users with market information.
* Support structured decision-making.

Users remain responsible for their own trading decisions.

---

# No Guaranteed Performance Incentives

LogiTrade does not design systems around promises of guaranteed profits or unrealistic performance expectations.

The platform is developed with the understanding that:

* Markets are unpredictable.
* Losses can occur.
* AI systems have limitations.
* Past performance does not guarantee future results.

User trust is prioritized over short-term incentives.

---

# AI Model Independence

LogiTrade uses artificial intelligence systems to analyze market information and support trading workflows.

Potential conflicts may arise if AI systems are influenced toward:

* Specific assets.
* Specific exchanges.
* Artificially favorable outcomes.
* Commercial incentives.

LogiTrade aims to maintain objective system design by focusing AI workflows on analysis, evaluation, and risk awareness.

---

# Asset and Trading Neutrality

LogiTrade aims to avoid unfair preference toward specific cryptocurrencies unless based on:

* Market analysis.
* System criteria.
* User-selected preferences.
* Defined platform logic.

The platform should not favor assets solely because of commercial relationships.

---

# Exchange Relationships

LogiTrade may interact with cryptocurrency exchanges or technology providers to provide platform functionality.

Potential relationships may include:

* API integrations.
* Technology partnerships.
* Service providers.
* Infrastructure providers.

Any external relationship should not compromise the integrity of platform operations.

LogiTrade aims to evaluate partnerships based on:

* Reliability.
* Security.
* User benefit.
* Technical suitability.

---

# Affiliate and Partnership Disclosure

LogiTrade may establish partnerships or business relationships that support platform growth.

Examples may include:

* Technology partnerships.
* Service integrations.
* Educational collaborations.
* Business development agreements.

Where applicable, material relationships should be disclosed transparently.

---

# Internal Trading Activities

If LogiTrade or related parties participate in cryptocurrency markets, potential conflicts may exist.

Examples include:

* Holding digital assets.
* Testing trading systems.
* Research activities.
* Internal platform evaluation.

LogiTrade aims to maintain appropriate separation between internal activities and user-facing services.

---

# User Data and Business Interests

LogiTrade recognizes that user information must not be used in ways that create unfair advantages.

LogiTrade does not intend to use user information for:

* Manipulating markets.
* Trading against users.
* Selling private user information.
* Creating unfair competitive advantages.

User data handling is governed by the Privacy Policy.

---

# Employee and Contributor Responsibilities

Individuals involved with LogiTrade should act responsibly and avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company responsibilities.

Potential conflicts may include:

* Personal investments affecting professional decisions.
* Undisclosed external relationships.
* Misuse of confidential information.

Team members should disclose relevant conflicts where appropriate.

---

# Intellectual Property Protection

LogiTrade develops proprietary technology, including:

* AI workflows.
* Trading intelligence systems.
* Research methodologies.
* Platform architecture.

Protecting intellectual property is important; however, intellectual property protection should not compromise transparency regarding user-facing operations.

---

# Transparency Commitment

LogiTrade aims to maintain transparency through:

* Clear documentation.
* Risk disclosures.
* Privacy policies.
* Service explanations.
* Responsible communication.

Users should have access to accurate information about platform capabilities and limitations.

---

# Conflict Management Process

Potential conflicts may be managed through:

* Internal review.
* Policy evaluation.
* Separation of responsibilities.
* Disclosure when appropriate.
* Removal of conflicting incentives.

The objective is to protect platform integrity and user trust.

---

# User Responsibility

Users should understand that:

* LogiTrade provides technology, not guaranteed outcomes.
* Cryptocurrency trading involves risk.
* External market conditions affect results.
* Users remain responsible for their decisions.

---

# Policy Updates

LogiTrade may update this Conflict of Interest Policy as the company, technology, and business relationships develop.

Updates may be made to improve:

* Transparency.
* User protection.
* Operational standards.
* Compliance practices.

LogiTrade believes trust is built through transparency, responsible development, and alignment between company operations and user interests.
